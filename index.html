Sandbox math

A backyard sandbox goes wrong in three places: the sand order that runs out one bag short, the frame that needs one more board than the car can carry, and the cover cut to the box instead of the box plus its overhang. Sandbox math turns the box into the liters and bags of play sand, turns the perimeter into the boards and corner posts, and turns the overhang into the cover fabric. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Sand plan - the box at a labeled bag size becomes the liters and the bags.
  • Frame plan - the perimeter at a labeled board length becomes the boards and the corner posts.
  • Cover plan - the box plus a labeled overhang becomes the cover area and the fabric strips.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: a 25 L bag of play sand, a 2.4 m frame board, a 0.25 m cover overhang each side and a 2 m fabric width - all illustrative defaults you can change by measuring. Measure your own materials and read the supplier's bag before you buy; this is planning arithmetic, not a materials list.
